# E29.14 current-literature novelty check — 2026-08-13

This check was run after E29.14b scoring. It therefore assesses positioning; it
did not select E29.14a/b prompts, masks, gates, or outcomes.

## Closest current work

- Haklay et al., *Position-aware Automatic Circuit Discovery* (ACL 2025,
  arXiv:2502.04577) makes circuit discovery position-sensitive and introduces
  semantic position schemas. It establishes that aggregating a component over
  positions can hide smaller faithful circuits.
- Vaidyanathan et al., *The Curse of Multiple Mediators: Hidden Interaction
  Effects in Activation Patching* (arXiv:2606.27510) proves that standard
  patching estimands contain context-dependent component interactions and that
  group interventions decompose into pairwise and higher-order terms. Their
  backup-head examples make large, conditional component sets scientifically
  important rather than a nuisance.
- Olivieri and Pérez Rodríguez, *Continuous-Depth Field Theory for Transformer
  Patching and Mechanistic Interpretability* (arXiv:2605.25225) treats a patch
  as a localized depth-token source and studies sensitivity/Green-operator
  propagation in a bounded local-linear regime.
- Szablewski and Masiak, *Activation Transport Operators* (arXiv:2508.17540)
  studies low-rank linear transport of SAE features across residual-stream
  depth.

## What is and is not distinct here

The ingredients are not new individually: activation patching, position-aware
circuits, interchange interventions, nonlinear interaction terms, and local
Jacobian propagation all have direct prior art. The defensible distinctive
combination is:

1. prospectively localize one exact residual update to changed lexical tokens,
   with forward/reverse, static-position, adjacent-component, and graded-dose
   controls;
2. show by exact paired interventions—not a gradient approximation or fitted
   feature model—that this position restriction preserves causal response
   under fixed 24- and 32-component compositions;
3. connect this localized state rewrite to the already measured nonlinear
   component-interaction landscape, then scale it along a balanced hub-off
   ladder where backup computation becomes visible.

I did not find a source making that exact micro-to-mesoscale causal bridge.
That is a search result, not proof of absence. The strongest framing is not
“positions matter” or “patches interact”; those are established. It is that a
semantically defined token-local intervention remains quantitatively
equivalent after composition with dozens of model components, which permits a
coarse interaction landscape to be reinterpreted at a precise state interface.

## Implication for the next experiment

The recent multiple-mediators result increases the value of the planned
balanced c36-c47 ladder: backup components are exactly where singleton
rankings can fail. The ladder should retain group interventions and report
conditional component associations, while avoiding claims that a descriptive
association uniquely identifies a component's individual mediated effect.
