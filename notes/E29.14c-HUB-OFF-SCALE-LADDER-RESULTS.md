## Registered verdict

Every registered scientific gate passed. Removing the layer-0 MLP hub and
patching only token positions whose lexical content differs remains an
accurate surrogate for patching every position, even when 36, 40, 44, or all
47 remaining transformer sublayers are composed. The agreement is not a
saturation artifact: the c36 interventions remain about 0.10 of a clean
endpoint away from completion, while the unique c47 intervention reaches
about 0.981 of the endpoint.

## What this means

E29.14a showed that one early MLP update acts mainly through the words that
change. E29.14b preserved this localization in 24- and 32-component
compositions. E29.14c now carries the same result through the entire remaining
network: a feature-level intervention restricted to the task-bearing token
states reproduces the whole-sequence intervention while dozens of sublayers
interact and the system approaches its backup endpoint.

This is evidence for a stable mesoscale causal interface: the important state
change is localized in token space, while the number and identity of downstream
components can vary substantially. It does not establish model or task
universality, nor does it identify which individual downstream components
implement the backup computation.

## Exploratory backup localization

A clearly post-hoc centered-pseudoinverse analysis used the balanced nested
mask geometry to nominate individual components. The top eight were all
attention sublayers: components `38, 42, 40, 36, 26, 22, 24, 32`, corresponding
to attention in layers `19, 21, 20, 18, 13, 11, 12, 16`. The first four ranked
at the top of every c36-to-c40, c40-to-c44, and c44-to-c47 transition. Their
leave-one-context-out top-eight frequency was `1.0`; the all-position versus
lexical coefficient correlations were `0.9970`, `0.9966`, and `0.9966` across
the three transitions. The overall top-eight-all-attention pattern has an
exploratory hypergeometric probability of about `0.00234` under uniform ranks,
but this is not a confirmatory p-value.

The design can interpolate centered row patterns by construction, and its
coefficients alias nonlinear interactions and background masks. These ranks
are therefore candidate generators, not identified causal effects. The next
experiment must directly add and remove the four leading late-attention
components on fresh contexts and compare them with same-layer MLP and earlier
attention controls.
