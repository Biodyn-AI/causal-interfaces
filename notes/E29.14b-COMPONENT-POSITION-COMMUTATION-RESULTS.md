## Registered verdict

Every registered claim passed. On the same large component masks, restricting
the intervention to token positions whose words differ reproduced the causal
response of patching every position. This held both for informative hub-off
masks and for the saturated hub-on controls, separately at c24 and c32 and in
the equal-scale joint tests. The result authorizes the predeclared larger-scale
hub-off ladder.

The careful name for the finding is **position-scope equivalence under
component composition**. It is not an algebraic claim that two mask
multiplications commute; that would hold by construction. The empirical claim
is that discarding all shared/static-position updates leaves the measured
large-composition causal response nearly unchanged.

All gaps are normalized by the symmetric clean endpoint; the registered upper
limit was `0.03`. Every one of the 12 contexts and four templates passed the
`0.05` mean-absolute-gap criterion at both scales and hub states. All 24 c24
hub-off masks, all 16 c32 hub-off masks, all 24 c24 hub-on masks, and all 32
c32 hub-on masks passed their per-mask criterion.

The all-position anchors behaved as required. Hub-on masks reached
`0.99768/0.99687` of the clean endpoint at c24/c32. Hub-off masks remained
meaningfully below the endpoint: their mean absolute endpoint distances were
`0.26780/0.14336`, with one-sided 95% lower bounds `0.24501/0.12243`. The
bridge therefore did not pass merely because every large intervention had
already saturated. The fresh singleton control also transferred: all-position
and lexical-only means were `0.99904/1.00420`, with lexical-versus-all gap
upper bound `0.01176`.

## Why this advances the project

E29.14a localized one early-MLP state rewrite to the changed words. E29.14b
shows that the same localization remains accurate after composing that rewrite
with 23 or 31 other transformer sublayer interventions. This joins the
microscopic statement “which token states are rewritten?” to the mesoscale
statement “how do dozens of components interact?” without replacing the
intervention by a fitted predictor.

The result supports treating the existing c24/c32 causal landscape as sparse
interactions over content-bearing lexical states rather than indiscriminate
whole-sequence updates. It does not yet show that the equivalence persists at
c36-c47, on another task or model, or for arbitrary component sets. The next
prospective experiment should therefore climb directly to c36, c40, c44, and
the unique c47 all-minus-hub intervention, with paired all-position checks and
enough hub-off masks to detect the backup transition.
