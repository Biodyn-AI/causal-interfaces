# E33.1 independent IOI position-scope replication

## Purpose and claim boundary

E29.14 established on GPT-2 Medium lexical-induction prompts that a patch
restricted to changed lexical-token positions could reproduce an all-position
patch, including under large sublayer compositions. E33.1 tests the same
position-scope claim on a different checkpoint and a meaningfully different
behavior: syntactic indirect-object identification (IOI) in GPT-2 Small.

The registered claim is restricted to the fixed sixteen-context panel. A pass
does not establish model universality, a minimal circuit, an abstract causal
variable, or a generally sufficient layer-0 MLP.

## Development/confirmation separation

Before this protocol was frozen, a four-context development pilot used GPT-2
Small with the names Alice--Rachel. It established feasibility and fixed the
conditions and thresholds. Descriptively:

- layer-0 MLP lexical-only versus all-position transfer differed by at most
  about `0.012` on the four forward contexts;
- shared/static and matched-static transfers were approximately zero;
- the coordinate-rolled, exactly norm-matched MLP control was approximately
  zero;
- the first 12 sublayers patched only at changed name positions transferred
  about `0.96--1.01` of the clean endpoint; and
- all 24 sublayers patched only at changed name positions transferred about
  `0.96--0.99`, with near-source full-vocabulary distributions.

The confirmation panel uses 64 different names, sixteen unseen prompt strings,
and four fixed templates. No confirmation response was available when the
protocol, gates, code, or source hashes were sealed.

## Frozen model and panel

- Model: `gpt2`, revision `607a30d783dfa663caf39e06633721c8d4cfcd7e`.
- Runtime: CPU, float64, eager attention, evaluation mode.
- Panel: sixteen contexts, four per template, with four globally unique names
  per context.
- Each base/donor pair differs at exactly three aligned name-token positions.
- Base and donor preserve the same IOI role structure while changing all name
  identities. The base answer is its non-repeated name; the donor answer is
  its corresponding non-repeated name.
- All rows are retained. If any pair lacks a positive clean counterfactual
  endpoint of at least `0.50`, or either direction is not pairwise-correct in
  all sixteen contexts, the panel is classified as clean-infeasible before any
  patched confirmation response is acquired.

## Fixed interventions

Every condition is run forward and reverse:

1. layer-0 MLP at all positions;
2. layer-0 MLP at only the three changed name positions;
3. layer-0 MLP at all shared/static positions;
4. layer-0 MLP at each of two response-blind, hash-selected static scopes of
   equal cardinality;
5. neighboring layer-0 attention at changed name positions;
6. an energy-matched control obtained by cyclically rolling the exact MLP donor
   delta by half the hidden width before insertion;
7. the first 12 attention/MLP sublayers at all positions and at changed name
   positions; and
8. all 24 sublayers at all positions and at changed name positions.

The energy control preserves the exact per-token Euclidean norm of the inserted
delta while destroying its coordinate identity. Static control scopes are
selected solely by SHA-256 of context identity, replicate, and token index.

## Registered measurements

The scalar transfer coordinate is the clean-endpoint-normalized change in the
source-target versus recipient-target logit margin. Zero is the clean recipient
and one is the clean source.

Full-distribution fidelity is

`1 - JS(patched, clean_source) / JS(clean_recipient, clean_source)`.

Thus zero is the recipient distribution and one is the source distribution.
Counterfactual pairwise wins and exact top-1 target counts are also retained.

Uncertainty uses 5,000 paired bootstrap replicates, resampling four contexts
with replacement inside each of the four template strata. All thresholds and
the exact conjunction are encoded in
`experiments/e33_ioi_scope_replication/protocol.py`.

## Primary gates in plain language

- The layer-0 MLP must transfer at least `0.60` in each direction.
- Lexical-only and all-position MLP transfer must differ by at most `0.04` at
  the one-sided 95% bootstrap upper bound.
- Shared/static and matched-static controls must remain near zero.
- The focal MLP must beat both neighboring attention and the energy-matched
  scrambled control by the fixed margins.
- The first-12 lexical patch must transfer at least `0.90` and remain within
  `0.05` of the all-position version.
- The all-24 lexical patch must transfer at least `0.92` and remain within
  `0.08` of the source endpoint.
- Full-vocabulary JS fidelity must reach `0.80`, `0.94`, and `0.95` for the
  MLP, first-12, and all-24 lexical patches respectively.

The registered verdict passes only if every encoded gate passes. Failed gates
are retained without threshold changes or prompt replacement.

## Query accounting

There are 32 clean examples and 352 patched examples: sixteen contexts times
eleven conditions times two directions, for 384 logical model examples total.
