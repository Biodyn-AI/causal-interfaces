## Registered verdict

The two central subclaims split:

- **Passed:** the fixed layer-0 MLP component is a highly reliable sufficient
  trigger for endpoint-level behavior at c24 and c32, and the explicit
  gate-shaped predictor passes the registered overall predictive gate.
- **Failed:** the frozen model does not adequately predict the residual
  hub-absent branch in absolute, context-centered terms. Consequently the full
  registered absorbing-decomposition claim does not pass.
- **Failed, as expected to be difficult:** the gate model does not beat the
  strongest matched non-gated capped ensemble across both scales.

This is a scientifically informative partial confirmation, not a full pass.

## Main quantitative results

### 1. The absorbing trigger replicated prospectively

Component 1, the layer-0 MLP residual update, was the unique best one-coordinate
classifier of endpoint-level outcomes on both held panels.

| Held scale | Ceiling accuracy | Ceiling F1 | Mean hub-on response / clean endpoint |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| c24 | 0.9861 | 0.9859 | 0.9971 |
| c32 | 0.9809 | 0.9856 | 0.9963 |

It ranked first among all 48 components at both scales, and every registered
mechanistic threshold passed. This is the third lexical cohort on which the
same fixed component exhibits the effect, after the independent E29.11 and
E29.12 panels used only for development.

### 2. The overall gate-shaped predictor passed

| Held scale | R2 | Median context R2 | Minimum context R2 | MSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| c24 | 0.8300 | 0.8171 | 0.2040 | 1.6860 |
| c32 | 0.8208 | 0.8436 | 0.2820 | 0.4871 |

All 12 contexts and all four template groups had positive R2 at both scales.
The model beat capped additive and the smooth MLP pointwise at both scales. At
c32 its MSE ratios were `0.270` and `0.447`, with bootstrap upper bounds
`0.497` and `0.705`; joint ratios were `0.551` and `0.682`, with upper bounds
`0.797` and `0.892`. Every registered overall predictive condition passed.

The clean-proxy version also passed and was effectively unchanged
(`R2=0.8318/0.8210`; MSE `0.989/0.999` times the primary), so the extra c48
calibration query was not needed for these predictions.

### 3. The residual hub-absent model improved error but failed its absolute gate

On hub-absent rows, adding the frozen MLP/additive shape reduced MSE relative to
the gate-only branch at both scales:

| Held scale | Gate+shape R2 | Gate-only R2 | MSE ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| c24 | -0.2477 | -0.5950 | 0.7823 |
| c32 | 0.1163 | -0.2974 | 0.6811 |

Thus both preregistered per-scale error-ratio thresholds passed. However, the
equal-scale joint conditional R2 was `-0.1100`, and the joint bootstrap MSE
ratio was `0.7488` with 95% interval `[0.5568, 1.0047]`. The registered branch
gate therefore failed on both its absolute-R2 and uncertainty conditions. The
near-miss confidence bound does not rescue the negative absolute fit: the
current branch-level saturation calibration is wrong on these new contexts.

### 4. The matched non-gated ensemble remains competitive

The explicit gate model's MSE was `1.068` times the matched capped ensemble at
c24 and `0.929` times it at c32. The equal-scale joint ratio was `1.0335`, with
95% interval `[0.9804, 1.0750]`. The separate predictive-frontier superiority
claim failed. The interpretable decomposition is therefore not currently a
strict accuracy improvement over the strongest matched black-box surrogate.

### 5. The hub is sufficient at these scales, not globally necessary

The registered report-only all-minus-hub intervention at c47 still reached
`0.9867` of the clean endpoint on average, versus `0.9933` for all 48
components. Their mean normalized difference was only `0.0065`. Other
components can therefore compensate when almost the whole network is patched.
The correct interpretation is a scale-dependent sufficient trigger, not a
globally necessary Boolean gate.

The analytic hub-centered pairwise star was also worse than its layer-0
attention control star at both scales. A fixed collection of hub pair
interactions is not the right residual model.

## Interpretation and next decision

The strongest robust result is now the prospective localization of a sharp
mesoscale boundary to one early MLP update: its inclusion almost deterministically
selects the endpoint branch across fresh prompts and large random interventions.
The failed part is the assumed universal formula for the branch left after that
split. The next experiment should keep the confirmed hub identity fixed, fit
separate hub-present and hub-absent saturation curves from source-only anchors,
and test their scaling toward the c47 backup regime. It should not repeat the
same global-mean decomposition or pursue the failed analytic star.
