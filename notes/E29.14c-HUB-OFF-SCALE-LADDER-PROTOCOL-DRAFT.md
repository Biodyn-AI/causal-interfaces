# E29.14c hub-off scale-scope ladder — prospective protocol draft

## Purpose and claim boundary

E29.14b authorized a hub-off scale promotion by prospectively showing that,
at c24 and c32, restricting activation patching to aligned lexical positions
reproduces the all-position response of large masks. E29.14c tests whether that
scope surrogate survives the transition toward an almost-complete model at
c36, c40, c44, and c47.

The primary claim is **scope equivalence under high-cardinality component
composition** on the fixed GPT-2-medium task and prompt panel. Two mechanistic
claims remain separate: c36 must retain non-endpoint response geometry for the
first rung to be informative, and the unique all-minus-hub c47 intervention
must reach the clean endpoint under both scopes to support backup compensation.
Endpoint saturation cannot by itself establish rich response geometry, and a
failure of an endpoint claim cannot erase an independently passing scope claim.

This experiment does not establish exact equality, necessity, a minimal
circuit, random-mask population inference, task generality, or model
generality. Literal component and position masks multiply commutatively by
construction; the empirical question is whether their two intervention scopes
produce equivalent model responses under composition.

## Frozen model, prompts, and intervention

- Use the exact E29.14b GPT-2-medium checkpoint, CPU-float64 eager runtime, and
  twelve prompts. Reusing the prompt panel isolates cardinality from lexical
  cohort transfer.
- Component 1, the layer-0 MLP update, is absent from every ladder mask.
- For every selected component, patch either all aligned positions or only
  aligned positions whose token identities differ, using the exact E29.14b
  donor-to-base hook and response definition.
- Repeat the component-1 singleton under both scopes as a run-local positive
  control. Repeat all 24 clean base/donor forwards before admitting labels.

## Response-blind balanced nested masks

The 47 non-hub components are placed in a fixed SHA-256 order under domain
`e29_14c_non_hub_order_v1|29143`. The panel contains 47 cyclic rows. Each row
omits 11 components at c36, seven at c40, three at c44, and none at c47. The
frozen residue shells are:

| Transition | Added residue offsets | Components added per row |
|---|---|---:|
| c36 to c40 | `10, 16, 29, 42` | 4 |
| c40 to c44 | `15, 18, 25, 45` | 4 |
| c44 to c47 | `0, 12, 26` | 3 |

Thus every row is nested as `c36 < c40 < c44 < c47`. Each non-hub component
appears exactly 36, 40, and 44 times in the corresponding 47-row panels. The
three transition incidence matrices are full rank and have fixed 2-norm
condition numbers below eight, allowing failures to be mapped to balanced
4/4/3 component shells without selecting masks from outcomes. Pair-incidence
imbalance, exact rows, component order, and the following canonical hashes are
sealed:

## Condition order and query budget

Each context has exactly 286 patched conditions in this order:

1. component-1 singleton at all positions, then lexical positions;
2. c36 rows 0--46, each paired all then lexical;
3. c40 rows 0--46 in the same paired order;
4. c44 rows 0--46 in the same paired order;
5. the unique c47 row, all then lexical.

This gives 3,432 patched context-condition cells plus 24 clean forwards, or
3,456 logical model examples. A complete context-0 batch-invariance smoke
repeats all 286 conditions at physical batch sizes 1 and 32. Its 572 examples
are not scientific labels, giving at most 4,028 physical model examples.

## Response and uncertainty

As in E29.14b, the raw patched response shift is divided by each context's
positive symmetric clean endpoint. Clean base is zero and clean donor is one.
For scale `s`, row `m`, and context `c`, define the signed scope gap

`delta[s,m,c] = r[lexical,s,m,c] - r[all,s,m,c]`.

All uncertainty uses 5,000 replicates and seed `29143`. Contexts are resampled
inside the four fixed three-context template strata. At c36--c44, chain rows
are also resampled while preserving all-versus-lexical pairs. The joint
bootstrap uses common row draws across the three nested panels and gives each
of c36, c40, c44, and c47 exactly one-quarter weight. The c47 claim uses only
context resampling because it has one possible hub-off mask. These intervals
are fixed-design sensitivity analyses, not random-mask population intervals.

## Registered gates

Clean correctness, finite and bounded outputs, exact hashes, environment,
write-once attempt custody, and complete batch invariance are hard integrity
requirements.

The singleton control repeats the E29.14b thresholds: each scope mean must be
in `[0.985, 1.01]`; each endpoint-gap bootstrap upper bound and the paired
scope-gap upper bound must be at most `0.03`.

For each of c36, c40, and c44:

- bootstrap upper mean `|delta|` is at most `0.03`;
- at least 11/12 context means and all four template means are at most `0.05`;
- at least 46/47 mask means are at most `0.05`;
- at least 536/564 individual cells have `|delta| <= 0.10`.

For c47, the same bootstrap, context, and template bounds apply; at least 11/12
cells must have `|delta| <= 0.10`. There is no mask-replication claim. The
equal-weight joint four-scale bootstrap upper bound must be at most `0.03`.
These per-scale gates, the joint gate, the singleton, and integrity authorize
the high-scale lexical-position surrogate.

Two separate endpoint claims are scored but do not redefine scope equivalence:

- **Informative c36 geometry:** the bootstrap lower bound on the mean absolute
  all-position distance from endpoint one is at least `0.03`.
- **c47 backup endpoint:** under each scope, the normalized mean lies in
  `[0.95, 1.01]` and the context-bootstrap upper mean absolute endpoint gap is
  at most `0.05`.

Monotonicity and scale correlations are descriptive, not hidden vetoes.

## Registered failure localization

The scorer preserves normalized all- and lexical-scope response geometry at
every scale. For each nested transition it also preserves the actual response
increments under both scopes,

`r[scope,next] - r[scope,current]`,

with raw 47-by-12 diagnostic matrices, row/context/template summaries, and the
exact added-component incidence rows. These increments answer which balanced
4/4/3 shells restore the endpoint. Separately, it reports

`delta[next] - delta[current]`,

which localizes where the lexical surrogate itself begins to diverge. These
are response-preserving diagnostics, not promotion gates or fitted component
claims. Any later sparse regression must be labeled post hoc and confirmed on
new masks before it becomes causal evidence.

## Decision

- If scope equivalence passes at every rung and jointly, lexical-only patching
  is authorized as a cheaper high-scale surrogate for this panel.
- If c36 non-saturation and c47 backup also pass, report an informative
  transition from residual geometry to near-full-network compensation.
- If the first scope failure occurs after a passing rung, use the corresponding
  fixed shell increments and scope-gap residuals to nominate components for a
  separately sealed confirmation.
- If c36 fails after E29.14b passed at c32, localize the boundary to the
  c32--c36 interval; do not infer an individual component from non-nested
  cross-experiment masks.
- If the singleton fails, stop and diagnose run integrity or positional
  transfer before interpreting the ladder.
