# E29.14b component-position commutation bridge — prospective protocol draft

## Purpose and claim boundary

E29.14a prospectively established on fresh words that replacing GPT-2-medium's
layer-0 MLP update only at differing lexical-token positions reproduces its
complete all-position effect. E29.14b asks whether that positional compression
survives composition with large component masks.

For an identical component mask, the experiment compares conventional
all-position activation patching with patching only aligned positions whose
input token identities differ between the base and donor prompts. A pass means
that the two intervention axes approximately commute on this model and task:
large-mask response geometry can be studied on the smaller lexical-position
support without materially changing its causal response.

This does not establish exact equality, natural-use necessity, task or model
generality, or a minimal circuit. It also does not validate a particular
predictor of the remaining hub-off response shape.

## Frozen independent inputs

- Model: the exact GPT-2-medium checkpoint and CPU-float64 eager runtime sealed
  by E29.14a.
- Prompts: twelve wholly new base/donor pairs, three under each of the four
  fixed templates, containing 96 distinct one-token group words disjoint from
  every E29.8--E29.14a group word.
- Masks: every row of the exact sealed E29.13 c24 and c32 held panels from
  `protocol_masks.pt`, SHA-256
  `014b87e0ddf2cf913ff745e41e12bb6444420716753d0eb712bd465eaa0c83fd`.
  No E29.13 response label or fitted prediction is available to registration,
  acquisition, or scoring.

The mask strata are fixed:

| Scale | Rows | Hub off | Hub on |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| c24 | 48 | 24 | 24 |
| c32 | 48 | 16 | 32 |

## Frozen intervention and order

For each component selected by mask `S`, recipient update `u_r`, clean donor
update `u_d`, and position indicator `p`, the hook returns

`u_r + p * (u_d - u_r)`.

Unselected components return `u_r`. The only scopes are `all_positions` and
`differing_lexical_positions`. Acquisition is forward donor-to-base only.

Each context has 194 fixed patched conditions in this exact order:

1. component-1 singleton at all positions and then differing lexical positions;
2. c24 rows 0--47, each immediately paired as all then lexical;
3. c32 rows 0--47, each immediately paired as all then lexical.

The singleton is a positive transfer control: it separates a failure of the
known E29.14a effect to transfer to the new cohort from composition-induced
failure. It is not fitted or selected on E29.14b outcomes.

## Response and paired discrepancy

For context `c`, mask `m`, and scope `s`, let

`r[s,m,c] = (patched donor-minus-base margin - clean-base margin) / A[c]`,

where `A[c]` is the positive symmetric clean endpoint: the sum of the base and
donor clean correct-target margins. Thus clean base is zero and clean donor is
one. The bridge discrepancy is

`delta[m,c] = r[lexical,m,c] - r[all,m,c]`.

## Uncertainty

All registered uncertainty uses 5,000 crossed-bootstrap replicates with seed
`29142`. Each replicate resamples contexts within the four fixed template
strata and masks within each scale-by-hub-state stratum, preserving each
all-versus-lexical pair. Registered upper bounds are empirical `q=0.95`
quantiles and registered lower bounds are empirical `q=0.05` quantiles.
Joint c24+c32 metrics give the two scales equal weight.

## Independent gates

Clean correctness, finite outputs, magnitude limits, exact prompt/mask/source/
checkpoint hashes, exact environment, immutable attempt custody, and the
batch-invariance smoke are mandatory integrity checks.

### Fresh singleton transfer control

- all-position and lexical-position mean responses are each in `[0.985, 1.01]`;
- the bootstrap upper bound on mean absolute endpoint gap is at most `0.03` for
  each scope;
- the bootstrap upper bound on mean absolute lexical-versus-all discrepancy is
  at most `0.03`.

### All-position anchors, separately at c24 and c32

- hub-on all-position mean response is in `[0.985, 1.01]`;
- its bootstrap upper mean absolute endpoint gap is at most `0.03`;
- the hub-off bootstrap lower bound on mean absolute distance from the endpoint
  is at least `0.05`, proving that the primary comparison is not made entirely
  inside the saturated endpoint branch.

### Primary hub-off bridge, separately at c24 and c32

- bootstrap upper mean `|delta|` is at most `0.03`;
- at least 11/12 context-wise mean absolute discrepancies are at most `0.05`;
- all four template-wise mean absolute discrepancies are at most `0.05`;
- at least 23/24 c24 masks and 15/16 c32 masks have across-context mean
  absolute discrepancy at most `0.05`.

### Hub-on saturation control, separately at c24 and c32

- the same discrepancy, context, and template gates as the hub-off bridge;
- at least 23/24 c24 masks and 31/32 c32 masks pass the `0.05` mask-wise bound;
- lexical-position mean response is in `[0.985, 1.01]` and its bootstrap upper
  mean absolute endpoint gap is at most `0.03`.

### Joint two-scale bridge

The equal-scale bootstrap upper mean `|delta|` is at most `0.03`, separately
for hub-off and hub-on strata.

The complete bridge passes only if integrity, singleton transfer, both anchors,
all four scale-by-hub equivalence gates, and both joint gates pass. A hub-off
scale ladder may still be promoted if every hub-off and integrity gate passes
but a hub-on saturation control fails; that outcome must not be called general
all-mask equivalence.

## Query accounting and custody

- 2 singleton scopes + 96 masks × 2 scopes = 194 patched conditions/context;
- 2,328 patched context-condition cells;
- 24 clean forwards;
- maximum 2,352 logical model examples.

The registered batch-invariance smoke repeats all 194 conditions for context 0
at physical batch sizes 1 and 32. Its 388 examples yield a maximum of 2,740
physical model examples and do not enter scientific labels.

Registration seals the entire condition manifest, new prompts and tokenizer
checks, exact prior-mask bytes, gates, bootstrap, query accounting, checkpoint,
runtime, protocol note, and complete local source closure before any E29.14b
model response. Acquisition writes an immutable attempt marker after verifying
the seal and before model loading; any marker blocks silent reruns. Labels and
success custody are also write-once. Scoring verifies the full chain and cannot
alter the registered gates.

## Next decision

- If the complete bridge passes, promote a pre-frozen hub-off scale ladder
  toward c36, c40, c44, and the unique c47 all-minus-hub mask using lexical-only
  and paired all-position checks.
- If only the hub-off bridge passes, promote that restricted ladder without a
  general equivalence claim.
- If singleton transfer passes but hub-off composition fails, use the paired
  discrepancies to localize which later components route information through
  static positions; do not use lexical-only patching as a scale surrogate.
- If singleton transfer fails, stop the bridge and relocalize on the new cohort.
