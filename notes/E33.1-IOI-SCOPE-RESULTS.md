# E33.1 independent IOI position-scope replication — results

## Verdict

**Every registered scientific gate passed.** On sixteen fresh, unfiltered
GPT-2 Small IOI context pairs, a layer-0 MLP donor patch restricted to the
three changed name-token positions reproduced its all-position causal effect.
The same position-scope equivalence held when the first 12 or all 24
attention/MLP sublayers were patched simultaneously. The result was
bidirectional and survived full-vocabulary distribution scoring, two matched
static controls, neighboring-attention control, and an exactly energy-matched
scrambled-direction control.

This is an independent checkpoint-and-behavior replication of the E29.14
position-scope phenomenon. It is not a claim of model universality, a minimal
circuit, or an identified abstract causal variable.

For MLP0, the mean absolute lexical-versus-all discrepancy was only `0.00488`

Both equal-cardinality hash-selected static controls remained negligible:
their largest mean absolute transfer was `0.00296`. MLP0 beat neighboring
attention by `0.62418/0.63321` and the exactly norm-matched coordinate-rolled
control by `0.72329/0.74226` forward/reverse.

## Mesoscale composition

The first-12 lexical patch differed from its all-position counterpart by mean
absolute `0.00937/0.01091`; bootstrap upper bounds were `0.01112/0.01387`
against the registered `0.05` limit.

The all-24 lexical patch remained within mean absolute `0.02159/0.02289` of
the clean source endpoint. Bootstrap upper bounds were `0.02602/0.02832`
against the registered `0.08` limit. All 24 lexical patches produced the
counterfactual pairwise answer in 16/16 contexts in both directions.

## Full-vocabulary distribution fidelity

Fidelity is

`1 - JS(patched, source) / JS(recipient, source)`,

so zero is the clean recipient distribution and one is the clean source.

| Intervention | Forward JS fidelity | Reverse JS fidelity |
|---|---:|---:|
| MLP0, changed names | 0.89593 | 0.92670 |
| First 12, changed names | 0.99006 | 0.99199 |
| All 24, changed names | 0.98987 | 0.99140 |

Thus the mesoscale result is not confined to the selected two-token logit
contrast: changed-name-only interventions reconstruct almost the complete
source next-token distribution.

## Clean behavior and boundaries

Every context was pairwise-correct in both directions and the minimum clean
endpoint was `10.554`. The intended clean answer was global top-1 in 11/16
contexts on each side; consequently, pairwise answer transfer is the primary
behavioral endpoint and exact top-1 counts are reported rather than silently
filtered.

The layer-0 MLP alone is strongly sufficient for the counterfactual pairwise
answer (16/16 each direction), but it transfers about 78--80%, not the nearly
complete singleton effect seen in GPT-2 Medium induction. The replicated
invariant is therefore **position scope**, not singleton effect size. Shared
positions add almost nothing, while the semantically changed name states
carry the effect through both a focal component and large component
compositions.
