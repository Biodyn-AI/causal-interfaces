# E29.14a positional lexical interchange — prospective protocol draft

## Purpose and claim boundary

E29.13 prospectively showed that replacing the complete layer-0 MLP residual
update at every prompt position is sufficient to move GPT-2-medium almost to
the clean donor endpoint. A post-outcome probe on those same prompts then
showed that the effect is bidirectional, concentrated at positions whose words
change between the prompts, and steep but continuous under interpolation.
Those localization results are development evidence only.

E29.14a tests the resulting hypothesis once on twelve wholly new lexical
contexts. It does not fit a predictor, search over components or positions, or
test larger component-mask cardinalities. The four claims below are scored
separately:

1. **Replication (R):** the complete layer-0 MLP interchange again reaches the
   clean counterpart endpoint.
2. **Lexical localization (L):** changing only aligned positions with different
   token identities reproduces the complete effect, while shared/static
   positions do not.
3. **Bidirectionality (B):** the same intervention works from base to donor and
   from donor to base.
4. **Graded dose (D):** interpolation produces an ordered intermediate response,
   contradicting a literal Boolean-gate interpretation.

A pass supports a component-specific early lexical-state interchange. It does
not by itself establish a globally necessary component, a minimal circuit, a
task-general mechanism, or a cross-model result.

## Frozen model and prompts

- Model: the same pinned GPT-2-medium revision and checkpoint bytes as E29.13.
- Runtime: CPU, float64, eager attention, evaluation mode.
- Prompts: 12 new base/donor pairs, three under each of the four fixed E29.11
  templates, containing 96 distinct group words disjoint from E29.8--E29.13.
- Response-blind tokenizer checks must establish one-token group words, equal
  aligned prompt lengths, distinct base/donor targets, and the registered
  semantic-position metadata below.
- Acquisition-time clean-model checks must establish positive clean correctness
  margins and revalidate the exact runtime semantic-position partition before
  any patched label is admitted.

## Frozen intervention

The focal component is component 1, the layer-0 MLP `c_proj` residual update.
Component 0, the layer-0 attention `c_proj` residual update, is the matched
control. For recipient update `u_r`, clean source update `u_s`, position mask
`m`, and dose `alpha`, the hook returns

`u_r + alpha * m * (u_s - u_r)`.

The fixed position scopes are:

- `all_positions`;
- `differing_lexical_positions`, where aligned base and donor token IDs differ;
- `shared_static_positions`, their complement;
- `demonstration_target_position`, the unique first-sequence answer token;
- `demonstration_prefix_positions`, differing positions before that answer;
- `repeated_query_positions`, differing positions after that answer;
- `differing_except_demonstration_target`.

Validation must prove that prefix, target, and repeated-query scopes are a
disjoint union of the differing-token scope.

## Frozen condition manifest and query budget

Each context has exactly 16 patched rows:

- forward donor-to-base, focal component, dose 1 at all seven scopes;
- forward donor-to-base, focal component, all positions at doses 0.25, 0.50,
  and 0.75 (dose 1 is already present and dose 0 is the clean base);
- reverse base-to-donor, focal component, dose 1 at all, differing, shared, and
  demonstration-target scopes;
- forward donor-to-base, control component, dose 1 at all and differing scopes.

Acquisition is one nonadaptive panel: 192 patched context-condition cells plus
24 clean forwards, at most 216 logical queries. No response is available to
registration or condition construction. No patched scientific response may
alter query selection or stopping; only a preregistered clean, numerical, or
batch-invariance integrity failure may abort acquisition before labels are
admitted.

The registered batch-invariance smoke repeats the full 16-condition manifest
on context 0 at physical batch sizes 1 and 32. Those 32 repeated condition
cells produce no scientific labels, giving a maximum of 248 physical model
examples while leaving the unique logical-query count at 216.

## Normalization and uncertainty

For each context, the forward response is the change in
donor-target-minus-base-target logit margin from the clean base prompt. The
reverse response is defined analogously from the clean donor prompt. Both are
divided by the same positive symmetric clean endpoint `A`, the sum of the two
clean correct-target margins.

Uncertainty uses 5,000 paired context bootstrap replicates with seed `29141`.
Each replicate independently resamples three contexts with replacement inside
each of the four fixed template strata, preserving paired conditions and equal
template weight. Registered upper bounds are the 0.95 empirical quantiles.

## Independent gates

Clean correctness, finite outputs, registered magnitude bounds, checkpoint and
source hashes, and the batch-invariance smoke are mandatory integrity checks.

### R — replication

- mean normalized forward all-position focal response is at least `0.985`.
- the 95% bootstrap upper bound on the mean absolute deviation of that
  response from the clean donor endpoint (`1.0`) is at most `0.03`.

### L — lexical localization

- the 95% bootstrap upper bound on the mean paired absolute difference between
  forward differing-position and all-position responses is at most `0.03`;
- mean absolute forward shared/static response is at most `0.02`.

Component specificity is reported as a separate control claim and passes when
the larger of the mean absolute component-0 all-position and differing-position
responses is at most `0.05`.

### B — bidirectionality

- mean normalized reverse all-position focal response is at least `0.985`;
- the 95% bootstrap upper bound on the mean absolute deviation of that
  response from the clean base endpoint (`1.0`) is at most `0.03`;
- the 95% bootstrap upper bound on the mean paired absolute forward-versus-
  reverse all-position difference is at most `0.04`.

The stronger phrase *bidirectional lexical interchange* additionally requires
the reverse differing-versus-all bootstrap upper bound to be at most `0.03`
and mean absolute reverse shared/static response to be at most `0.02`.

### D — graded dose

- aggregate forward all-position means are strictly ordered from the derived
  clean-recipient response through all registered doses:
  `alpha 0 < 0.25 < 0.50 < 0.75 < 1.00`;
- at least 10 of 12 contexts have the same strict ordering;
- the mean response at dose 0.50 lies strictly between `0.20` and `0.80`.

Target, prefix, repeated-query, target-excluded, and nonadditivity contrasts are
descriptive localization outputs, not extra hidden pass conditions.

## Custody and decisions

Registration seals the prompt records and checks, condition manifest, gates,
bootstrap, query arithmetic, checkpoint hashes, development-artifact hashes,
and every acquisition/scoring source hash before an E29.14a model response.
Acquisition verifies those values and writes an immutable attempt marker before
loading the model. Any attempt marker blocks a silent rerun, including after an
integrity failure or process crash; a replacement attempt would require an
explicitly registered deviation. Acquisition then executes all conditions
without scoring or adaptation, repeats clean and numerical gates, and writes
one immutable label artifact plus custody hash. Scoring verifies registration,
attempt, and label custody and writes one immutable summary.

- If R, L, B, D, and the component control pass, describe a prospectively
  confirmed, component-specific, bidirectional lexical-state switch with a
  graded dose response—not an abstract Boolean gate. The exact curve shape is
  descriptive unless separately gated.
- If R and L pass but D fails, lexical interchange is confirmed while dose
  semantics remain unresolved.
- If R passes but L fails, retain all-position semantics and do not impose a
  lexical-only scope on later mask scaling.
- If forward replication passes but B fails, use sufficiency language only.
- If the component control fails, run a broader matched-component screen before
  asserting component specificity.
- If R fails, stop the planned scale ladder and relocalize the phenomenon.

Before any later lexical-only multi-component c36--c47 experiment, a separate
paired bridge must compare all-position and lexical-only patching on fixed
hub-off c32 masks. Singleton equivalence does not imply whole-mask equivalence.
